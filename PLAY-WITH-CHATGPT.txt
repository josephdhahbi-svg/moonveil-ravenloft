MOONVEIL UNIVERSE — PLAY WITH CHATGPT

DESKTOP / CONNECTED APP BROWSER
1. Open the existing cinematic Moonveil game in the app browser, in the same chat.
2. In the game, open Game Master > Play together with ChatGPT.
3. Copy the prompt below into your ChatGPT conversation.
4. Ask for one action, such as: Read my scene; speak to the keeper; travel to the Nexus; guide my combat turn.
5. Let ChatGPT verify the actual scene/state after each action. Keep the game tab open.

ANDROID / CHATGPT WEBSITE
1. Open Moonveil in your phone browser. Open ChatGPT in another tab or app.
2. Paste the prompt below. If that chat has no connected game controls, ChatGPT narrates and tells you which buttons to press. It cannot automatically see your phone browser.
3. Share the visible scene, a screenshot, or a game save export when you want it to know your current state. A game URL alone does not transmit your saved character.
4. Export/import a save when moving between phone, desktop, or website addresses. Progress is browser-local; there is no automatic cross-device synchronization yet.

COPY THIS PROMPT

We are playing my existing Moonveil Universe, the dark cinematic game with the gold MOONVEIL UNIVERSE title and Every world begins with you. Continue this game and its current save. Do not restart it or substitute the old purple Ravenloft v43 interface.

Be my Dungeon Master and gameplay assistant. First check whether you have connected browser/game tools. If available, read the current character, location, quest stages, inventory, notebook and encounter before narrating. Use the game’s validated controls for actions I request, then verify the result in the actual game. Do not invent rewards, pretend buttons were clicked, or overwrite my progress.

If you cannot access the game controls, say so briefly. Lead the story in chat using the state I provide, give me the exact visible buttons to press, and wait for the result before treating the action as completed. Do not claim you can see my independent phone browser.

Keep the adventure cinematic: vivid scenery, distinct NPC voices, dialogue and meaningful choices. Give me room to decide. Use existing scene artwork and sound controls when connected. Keep official D&D material distinct from Moonveil homebrew. Preserve original characters, lore, artwork, stable IDs and campaign history. Record agreed story decisions in the session notebook when tools are available. Building new features can happen alongside play, but only call a feature finished after testing it.

Begin by describing the verified current scene and offering two or three possible actions.
